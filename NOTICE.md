# Scope, attribution, and licensing

This repository contains the project website and supporting release tools.
The three public interactive examples and the homepage bag illustration are
fictional explanations of the experimental design, not republished benchmark
pages or observations of model behavior.

WebContrast is derived from Mind2Web pages. Mind2Web declares its dataset under
CC BY 4.0 and separately requests that unzipped test data not be redistributed
online to reduce contamination. Upstream rights and any third-party rights are
not replaced by adding annotations. This website does not redistribute the
upstream page corpus and does not imply upstream endorsement.

- Dataset source: https://github.com/OSU-NLP-Group/Mind2Web
- CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Public-source licensing for the authors' website/code and for the annotation
layer will be selected by the authors and documented separately. Until an
explicit license is added, public visibility must not be described as an
unrestricted open-source grant. No upstream dataset is relicensed by this file.

No paper PDF is included. The paper title is a working research title; the site
makes no claim of conference acceptance or an existing arXiv identifier.
